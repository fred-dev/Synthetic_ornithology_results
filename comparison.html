Synthetic Ornithology Comparison Samples

These examples present side-by-side comparisons between recorded Australian soundscapes and outputs generated by EAGLE. Each pair uses the same or closely matched conditioning context, allowing the generated audio to be heard against a corresponding archival recording.

The purpose of these comparisons is not to test whether the model perfectly reproduces a specific place or species. Instead, they provide a way to listen for acoustic correspondence, perceptual fidelity, and the limits of the model’s ability to render a coherent ecological world from conditioning data.
