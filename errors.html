Synthetic Ornithology — Limits and Aberrations

These examples document moments where EAGLE’s generated soundscapes become unstable, producing audible artefacts, misaligned acoustic features, or behaviours that fall outside the expected world schema.

These outputs are not presented simply as failed examples. Within Synthetic Ornithology , model breakdowns reveal the limits of the system’s learned correspondences between conditioning data, species vocalisations, background textures, and ecological plausibility. They make the boundary of the rendered world audible.
