Synthetic Ornithology — Notable Outputs

These examples document recurring behaviours observed in EAGLE’s generated soundscapes. Rather than presenting these outputs only as errors, this page treats them as moments where the model’s learned sonic vocabulary becomes audible: species calls shift, patterns recombine, backgrounds blur, and ecological plausibility becomes unstable.

In the context of Synthetic Ornithology, these behaviours are significant because they reveal the boundary between adherence to the world schema and speculative variation. Some outputs remain perceptually convincing, while others expose the model’s internal recombination of acoustic features.
